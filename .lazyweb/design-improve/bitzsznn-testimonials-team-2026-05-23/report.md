# Bitzsznn testimonial and team polish

Date: 2026-05-23

Lazyweb MCP tools were not exposed in this session, so this pass used the user's supplied screenshots as the primary visual reference and applied the Lazyweb review checklist manually: clearer hierarchy, fewer competing layouts, stronger proof density, richer hover feedback, and cleaner CTA language.

## Applied improvements

1. Standardized testimonials around one premium proof-card format: large quoted statement, compact portrait, pagination dots, and clear left/right controls.
2. Removed the competing image-left testimonial layout so homepage, services, and about use the same pattern.
3. Expanded About team from 4 to 6 members with image-led cards.
4. Added hover and focus-within social popups on every About team card, using rounded icon buttons for Instagram and X.
5. Improved SEO/conversion copy around About and Services: clearer terms around NYSC events, youth culture, brand activations, community experiences, creators, and partnerships.

## Design notes

- Keep testimonials quiet and credible: the card now feels closer to editorial social proof than a carousel of disconnected mini cards.
- Team cards are intentionally image-first, with the contact affordance always visible and the social actions revealed on hover/focus.
- The testimonial card uses one active story at a time, which makes the proof easier to read and avoids the “inactive testimonial page” feeling.
